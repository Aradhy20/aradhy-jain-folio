## 2. Local Market Architecture: Noida & Delhi NCR

### Why Noida?
- Major industrial, manufacturing, and tech export hub (Sectors 57, 58, 62, 63, 67, 80, 83, 85, Phase II, Greater Noida).
- Home to hundreds of B2B manufacturing companies (metal, apparel, packaging, industrial tools) wanting modern web catalogs to attract national and international buyers.
- Concentration of higher education institutions (Knowledge Park I, II, III in Greater Noida).
- Dense startup ecosystem seeking cost-effective freelance engineering over expensive digital agencies.

### Honest Representation Boundaries
1. **No Fake Google Business Profile:** We do not publish fictitious physical office addresses or spoofed local listings.
2. **True Modality:** Aradhy operates as an independent freelance developer based in Noida, serving clients via organized remote sprints and direct on-site alignment meetings across Noida and Delhi NCR when required.
3. **Transparent Pricing:** Clear indicative ranges (₹15,000–₹1,20,000) with written milestones, zero markup on hosting, and full source code ownership.
